My favorite place

Mount Fuji

Mount Fuji is Japan's highest peak, standing at 3,776 meters (12,389 feet).
Located on Honshu Island southwest of Tokyo, this active stratovolcano is
globally recognized for its remarkably symmetrical, snow-capped cone.

Monaco City

Monaco is a tiny, independent city-state located on the French Riviera in Western Europe.
It is the second-smallest sovereign country in the world,
renowned globally for its immense wealth, high concentration of millionaires,
and tax-haven status.
